# 只给数据 · 能不能具体到学科名词

同一张统计卡、同一个模型（deepseek-v4-flash），三种问法。自由文本沿用 07 的 5 个种子；另外两个条件各只跑 1 次，不跑种子。

| 条件 | 问法 |
|---|---|
| 07 自由文本 | 原样："识别每个场最可能代表的物理量" |
| 强制学科名 | 同一问法，但明确禁止 concentration / density / amplitude / 扩散标量这类通用词，要求给出某个学科里命名的具体量 |
| 候选表 | 给 20 项学科级候选（生态、细胞、物理、化学、地球、材料、交通），要求排序 top-5 |

## 结果

| 条件 | 具体学科名 top-1 | top-2/3 内命中 | 宏观家族正确 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 07 自由文本（30 条） | 0/30 = 0% | — | 25/30 = 83% |
| 强制学科名（6 条） | 1/6 = 17% | 3/6 = 50% | 6/6 = 100% |
| 候选表（6 条） | 1/6 = 17% | 2/6 = 33% | 4/6 = 67% |

候选表随机基线：top-1 = 5%，top-3 = 15%。

## 逐条

| 真值 | 强制学科名（rank1，备选） | 候选表 top-5 |
|---|---|---|
| population density | fractional vegetation cover (alternatives: degree of saturation, tumor viability fraction) (evidence: starts near zero, evolves to fully occupied state, spectrum dominated by large-scale mode, monotone growth to 1.0) | phase field > order parameter > chemical species concentration > morphogen concentration > water content |
| morphogen concentration | population density of an invasive species in a reaction-diffusion model (alternatives: neural activation field in a spreading depression model, flame front temperature in combustion) (evidence: nonnegative, bell-shaped pulse spreading from x≈-0.78 to 0.62 with sharp fronts, low background, and spectral energy concentrated at low k, consistent with a traveling pulse/wave) | morphogen concentration > chemical species concentration > nutrient concentration > population density > phase field |
| order parameter | amplitude of a nonlinear Schrödinger soliton mode (alternatives: order parameter in a Ginzburg-Landau model, envelope of a modulated Rossby wave) (evidence: oscillatory growth localized in x, spectral peak at k=3 with drift to higher k, sign changes and asymmetric amplitude growth) | phase field > order parameter > morphogen concentration > chemical species concentration |
| traffic density | soil moisture content (alternatives: population density of a spreading species, volume fraction of a reactive phase) (evidence: strictly positive 0.1–0.7, localized growing bump with slow diffusion-like spread and near-zero background, no negative values) | morphogen concentration > chemical species concentration > nutrient concentration > population density > phase field |
| prey density | nutrient concentration in a chemostat (alternatives: resource density in predator-prey model, substrate concentration in biofilm model) (evidence: starts near zero, grows monotonically to saturation ~1, low spatial modes dominate, no negative values, typical of logistic/resource filling) | phase field > order parameter > chemical species concentration > morphogen concentration > nutrient concentration |
| predator density | consumer/biomass density in a reaction-diffusion system (alternatives: pathogen load, prey mortality indicator) (evidence: transient pulse then decay, small positive values, localized spatial structure, anticorrelated with resource uptake over time, decaying second-half drift) | chemical species concentration > morphogen concentration > nutrient concentration > sediment concentration > bacteria density |

## 三个观察

1. **不是不会说学科名词。** 被强制之后模型立刻给出 soil moisture content、fractional vegetation cover、nutrient concentration in a chemostat、consumer/biomass density 这类学科级名字，说明词汇层不是瓶颈。
2. **但只有生态/资源-消费者这一族被它猜中。** 六个场里唯一在 rank1 命中的是 Predator-Prey f2（真值 predator density，模型答 consumer/biomass density，rank1 命中）；Predator-Prey f1 （prey density）在 rank2 答成 resource density in a predator-prey model，也等于说对了角色；FSH 的 order parameter 排在第 2 位。也就是说，两场结构 + 一增一减的时间行为让模型唯一一次摸到了正确的机制族，其余单场问题它给的是植被覆盖度、土壤含水量这类同样合理但不对的名词。
3. **给候选表反而更差。** 20 项清单把模型锚在物理词上：phase field 成了 3/6 个场的 rank1，而 traffic density / prey density / predator density 一次都没进 top-5。开放但被强制时 top-2 命中 3/6，给菜单时 top-3 只有 2/6 —— 菜单缩小了它的推理空间，没有提高准确率。

结论：学科级名词模型是能给出的，缺的不是表达能力，而是**能把 population density 和 morphogen concentration 分开的信息**。也就说类似规律的学科可能混淆，不具备判别不同学科的能力，原因是统计卡里没有这类判别信息，所以它只能在几个同样自洽的学科解释之间挑，挑中与否基本由问题结构决定（两场耦合的那一例猜中，单场的那四例全错）。
